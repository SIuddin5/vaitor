How to add your real files

1. Upload your PDF, PPTX, DOCX or ODS files to a free service (Google Drive, Dropbox, or GitHub).
2. Get the public share / download link.
3. Replace the href="#" in this HTML file with your real download links.
4. Optionally place files inside an assets/ folder and link them relatively (e.g. assets/project-report.pdf).
